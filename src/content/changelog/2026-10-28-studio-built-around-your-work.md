The Studio's home page is now *Your work*: for a project, the entries you started that aren't live, publish requests waiting for review, what is scheduled (and why a blocked schedule stopped), and the latest releases. Inside a collection, *Only mine* narrows the list to the entries you started.

From a collection's list you can pick several entries and publish them together as one release, or send them as one publish request for review if your role can't publish. An entry can be duplicated, an earlier version restored with *Restore this version* (it becomes the newest version, nothing is lost), and a live entry taken down.

What changed for every screen:

- refusals come from the API's reasons and are said in plain words, with what to do next;
- controls follow your role's permissions, and navigation shows only what you can read;
- a release's receipt says what needs attention and the safe next step;
- reviewing and publishing work on a phone;
- Settings has a left panel of pages (General, Roles, Website connections, API tokens, Activity), and making something opens a dialog;
- the entry page says where the entry stands once, in one line naming its release;
- *About Colophon* in the account menu shows which server and version you are using, for a problem report.
