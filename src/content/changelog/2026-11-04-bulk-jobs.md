`POST /api/v1/projects/{project}/environments/{environment}/bulk` takes a manifest for one collection: up to 100 items and 1 MiB a chunk, up to 10,000 items a job. Each item names a stable `key`, the `entry`, the `expected_revision` the change is based on, and an `op`: `update` with new content, `set` one field, `move` a value, or `delete`. Later chunks go to `…/bulk/{job}/items` under `If-Match`, and `seal` starts the run.

Why it matters: a worker applies each item in its own transaction, as the person or token that submitted it, rechecking permissions and the base revision. An entry edited since gets `conflict`, never an overwrite. Outcomes are `applied`, `conflict`, `refused` or `skipped`, and a retried chunk adds nothing twice. Revoking the submitting token, or removing the member, stops the run. An interrupted client reads `GET …/bulk/{job}/items` in key order and carries on from there; `POST …/bulk/{job}/cancel` skips what is still pending.

Bulk jobs change drafts only; publishing stays a publish request.

```json
{"collection": "posts", "seal": true, "items": [
  {"key": "retag-1", "entry": "<entry id>", "expected_revision": "<revision id>",
   "op": {"op": "set", "path": "section", "value": "News"}}
]}
```
