A **Link to entries** field points from one entry to others: a recipe's *Goes well with*, an article's
author. This page is for writers who fill those fields, and explains the rules that keep links from
breaking on your site.

## Add a link field

Owners and developers add one in the content type, under **Content types** → the type → **Add a
field** → **Link to entries** (*Points to entries of another type, like an author.*). They choose:

- **Links to**: the content type whose entries it holds. It can be the same type.
- **How many**: **One entry**, or **A list of up to** a number between 1 and 100.
- **Mirrored on … by** (optional): a field on the other type that keeps the link back. See
  [Two-way links](#two-way-links).

## Choose entries

<Steps>

1. In the entry editor, find the field. Its search box says what it searches, for example *Search
   Recipes by dish*.

2. Type part of the title. Matching entries are listed with their status: **Draft**, **Live** or
   **Live with changes**; one already chosen says **Chosen**.

3. Press one to add it. In a list, add as many as the field allows.

</Steps>

![The Goes well with field: Lemon curd chosen, with move up, move down and remove buttons, and the search box with "tart" typed, listing Pear tart (Draft), Gooseberry tart (Draft) and Lemon tart (Live)](/screens/studio/link-picker.webp)

Chosen entries show under the field label. Use the arrows to change their order and the cross to
remove one. Removing a link doesn't change the other entry, unless the field is
[mirrored](#two-way-links). If a chosen entry no longer exists, it shows
**Not found**: remove it.

If your role can't read the type the field links to, the field says *Your role can't see Recipes, so you
can't choose one here.*

## Linked from

On the **Content** tab of a linked entry's editor, under its fields, **Linked from** lists every entry
that links to it. It isn't repeated on the other tabs:

![Linked from, Entries whose fields link to this one: Lemon tart, Recipes, in Goes well with, marked Readers see this link](/screens/studio/linked-from.webp)

Each line names the entry, its content type and the field, and says whether the link is published:
**Readers see this link** when the linking entry's live version holds it, **In a draft** when only a
draft does. An entry your role can't read shows as *An entry you can't see*.

## Two-way links

**Linked from** is read-only: it shows links, but you change them on the entry that holds them. For a
link both sides edit, the content type's field can be **mirrored**: a recipe's *Goes well with* kept in
step with the same field on the other recipe, or a book's *Author* with the author's *Books*. A save on
one side writes the other side in the same change, so the two never disagree. To offer a mirror, the
other type needs a link field back first.

## Why a linked entry can't go from under you

Colophon never lets readers get a link to an entry they can't get. That gives three rules.

### Publishing an entry that links to a draft

You can't publish an entry that links to an entry readers don't have. The publish stops and names it:
*“Lemon tart” links to “Lemon curd”, which isn't published yet. Publish “Lemon curd” first, or in the
same publish request.* On the publish request, **Make a publish request that includes “Lemon curd”**
builds one with both.

### Taking down an entry that a published entry links to

You can't take down an entry while a published entry links to it. Its side panel warns before you try:
*Readers have an entry that links here, so it can't be taken down until that changes.* Pressing
**Unpublish** asks first:

![Readers still have links to this: “Lemon tart” links to it, and readers have it. Colophon won't take this down until that changes: change or take down that entry first, or in the same publish request. Keep it, or Try anyway](/screens/studio/still-linked.webp)

**Keep it** leaves everything as it is. **Try anyway** sends the request, and Colophon refuses it if
the link is still published: *“Lemon curd” is still linked from “Lemon tart”, which readers have.
Change or take down “Lemon tart” first, or in the same publish request.* Either publish Lemon tart without the link
first, or take both down in one publish request.

Links in drafts don't block anything: only what readers have counts.

### Deleting an entry that is linked

Deleting is done through the management API (the Studio has no delete button yet: see
[Writing and autosave](/studio/writing/#deleting-an-entry)). It is refused with `409` while any entry
links to it, in a draft or published: *other entries still link to this one: remove those links first,
then delete it*. The answer lists the linking entries your role can read.

## What your website gets

A link is stored as the linked entry's id, or a list of ids, in the order you chose. The delivery API
only ever returns links to entries that are published in the same release, because publishing refuses
anything else.
