Everyone who works in a project is a **member** with one **role**, and the role decides what they may
do there. This page is for the project's owners, who invite people and decide what each role may do.

Open **Members** in the sidebar, under **People**.

![The Members page: Akira Shirase (you), Owner, with Leave project; Kai Tan, Publisher; Mina Ito, Editor; each with a role menu and Remove; and a pending invitation, Copy editor, Editor, with Revoke](/screens/studio/members.webp)

## Invite someone

<Steps>

1. Press **Invite a member**.

2. Under **Who is it for?**, write something that helps you recognise the invitation later (*Only you
   see this*), and choose the **Role** they will have. The role's description shows under it.

   ![The Invite a member dialog: Who is it for?, Copy editor; Role, Editor, Writes and edits content; and the Create invitation link button](/screens/studio/invite.webp)

3. Press **Create invitation link**.

4. **Copy** the link and send it to them yourself, by email or chat. *It is shown only now, works once,
   and expires on its own*, after 72 hours.

   ![Invitation ready: Send this link to Copy editor. It is shown only now, works once, and expires, with the date and time, the link and a Copy button](/screens/studio/invitation-ready.webp)

</Steps>

The invitation waits under **Pending invitations**, with its role and when it expires, until it is
used, revoked or expires. **Revoke** cancels it at once. Colophon sends no email itself.

The person opens the link. If they aren't signed in, they sign in, or create an account with **Create
one**, and the page then shows the invitation: *Akira Shirase invited you to join Field notes as
Editor: Writes and edits content.* They press **Join**, or **Don't join**. A link that was used, revoked
or expired says *This invitation has expired, was revoked, or was already used. Ask the person who sent
it for a new one.*

Inviting needs a role that may **Invite people and change their roles** (the Owner, by default), and
you can only offer a role whose every power your own role has.

## The built-in roles

Every project starts with four roles:

| Role | May |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything. Read, create, edit and delete content; approve and publish; change content types and review stages; manage website connections, members, roles and API tokens; read the activity log; manage the site's audience. The Owner role never changes. |
| **Developer** | Read, create and edit content; change content types and review stages; manage website connections. Not approve, publish or delete. |
| **Publisher** | Read, create and edit content; approve publish requests and publish them. |
| **Editor** | Read, create and edit content, and ask for review. |

In practice: an **Editor** writes and presses **Ask for review**; a **Publisher** reviews, approves and
publishes, and presses **Publish** on their own work; a **Developer** builds content types and connects
the website; an **Owner** runs the project. Deleting content is only possible through the API, and of
the built-in roles only the Owner may.

## Roles

Open **Settings** → **Roles** to see every role, how many people hold it, and to change them.
**Developer**, **Publisher** and **Editor** can be edited; the **Owner** can only be viewed. Changing
roles needs a role that may **Make and change roles** (the Owner, by default).

### Make your own role

<Steps>

1. Press **New role**. Give it a **Name** (*For example Copy desk, Translator or Guest writer.*); its
   **Short name** is made from the name, and can't change later. Press **Create and choose what it may
   do**.

2. Under **What it's for**, describe it. *Shown when someone is invited with this role.*

3. Under **Content**, tick what it may do. Each row is **Everything** (*every content type, including
   ones added later*) or one content type; each column is a kind of work: **See** (read drafts),
   **Create**, **Edit**, **Approve** (*OK someone's publish request*), **Publish** (*put it live*) and
   **Change its fields**.

4. Add **Limited permissions** for anything narrower than a whole column: see below.

5. Under **The whole project**, tick powers that aren't about one content type: **Invite people and
   change their roles**, **Make and change roles**, **Manage where releases go**, **Manage API tokens**,
   **See the activity log**.

6. Press **Save role**.

</Steps>

![The Recipe tester role: See and Create ticked for Recipes, two limited permissions (Edit: Recipes, only their own entries; Edit: Recipes, only Minutes), the form to add a limit, and The whole project's powers, all unticked](/screens/studio/role-editor.webp)

### Limited permissions

*Narrower than the grid: only some fields, or only the entries a member created themselves.* Choose
**What** (**See**, **Create** or **Edit**) and **Where** (**Every content type** or one), then tick:

- **Only their own entries**: the permission covers only entries the member created. For example, a
  guest writer who may edit their own recipes and nobody else's.
- **Only these fields**: the permission covers only the fields you tick. None ticked means every field.

Press **Add this limit**. The example role above may create recipes, edit every field of its own
recipes, and correct only the **Minutes** of anyone's.

In the editor, a member sees what their role allows: fields they may not see are hidden and never sent
with their saves, and fields they may read but not change are shown read-only, with *(your role can't
change it)* after the label.

You can never give a role more than your own role holds. Changing a role takes effect for everyone who
holds it. If someone else saved the role while you were editing, your save is refused and offers **Load
the latest version**.

### Delete a role

**Delete role…**, at the end of a role's page, removes it. It is refused while anyone holds the role or a
pending invitation offers it: *People still hold this role, or invitations offer it. Give them another
role first.* That goes for the built-in roles too: *Built in: you can change it, but not delete it while
anyone holds it.* The Owner role can never be deleted.

## Change someone's role

On **Members**, choose a new role in the person's **Role** menu. It applies at once, to everything they
do next.

## Remove someone

Press **Remove** beside the person. They lose access to the project at once; what they wrote stays
in the project. To leave a project yourself, press **Leave project** beside your own name.

### The last owner

A project always keeps at least one owner. Removing the last owner, changing their role, or the last
owner leaving is refused: *A project must keep at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first.*

Every change to members, invitations, roles and API tokens is written to **Settings** → **Activity**,
which nobody can edit or delete.
