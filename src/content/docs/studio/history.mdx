Every save makes a new version of an entry, and Colophon never writes over an old one. The **History**
tab lists them all, so a writer can see what changed and bring an earlier version back.

## See an entry's versions

Open the entry and press **History**. The number on the tab is how many versions it has.

The newest version is at the top, marked **Current draft**. The version readers get is marked with its
release, for example **Live in Release 2**. Each line shows the entry's title as it was then and when it was saved; hold the pointer
over *Saved 4 minutes ago* to see the exact time.

## Compare a version with the draft

<Steps>

1. Press **Compare with draft** on an older version.

2. The comparison opens under it: each field that differs, with the older value struck through and the
   draft's value beside it, and how many fields are unchanged.

3. Press **Hide** to close it.

</Steps>

![The History tab: the Current draft at the top, then the version that is live, compared with the draft: Minutes 45 struck through and 55 in the draft, 4 other fields unchanged, with Hide and Restore this version](/screens/studio/history.webp)

The comparison is with what is on your screen now, including changes still being saved.

## Restore an older version

<Steps>

1. Wait until the side panel says **Saved**: restoring is offered only when your changes are saved.

2. Press **Restore this version** on the version you want back.

3. Its fields fill the editor and are saved at once, as a **new** version on top of the current one.

</Steps>

The editor confirms: *Restored the version saved 4 minutes ago, as a new version. The newer ones stay in
the history.* Nothing is lost, so a restore can itself be undone by restoring the version before it.

Restoring changes the draft only. If the entry is live, readers keep getting the published version
until you publish again: see [Publishing and review](/studio/publishing/).

Fields your role can't change keep their current values, and fields your role can't see are never
sent, so a restore never touches what you aren't allowed to edit. If every field is read-only for you,
**Restore this version** isn't shown.

:::note[Restoring a whole release]
History works on one entry. To put back everything an earlier release published, use
[Rollback](/studio/rollback/).
:::
