Comments and tasks are how people working on the same content talk about it inside the Studio. A
**comment** says something about an entry as it is now. A **task** is a piece of work on an entry, with
someone it waits on and a status.

## Comments

Every entry has a **Comments** tab beside **Content** and **History**. The number on the tab counts the
comments not yet answered.

<Steps>

1. Open the entry and press **Comments**.

2. Type in the box (*Say what needs changing or checking…*), up to 8,000 characters.

3. Press **Comment**.

</Steps>

![The Comments tab: a comment being written, the All, Open and Answered filters, an open comment from Mina Ito with Resolve, and an answered comment from Kai Tan, Answered by Mina Ito, with Reopen](/screens/studio/comments.webp)

### Comments are pinned to a version

*It attaches to the version on screen and can't be edited.* A comment records the exact version of the
entry its author was looking at, so what was said always stays tied to what was there. When the entry
is saved again, older comments say *on an earlier version*: open the **History** tab to see that
version (see [History and restore](/studio/history/)).

A comment can't be edited or deleted. To correct one, write another.

### Resolve and reopen

- **Resolve** marks a comment answered. It shows **Answered**, by whom and when, and leaves the **Open**
  list.
- **Reopen** puts an answered comment back in the **Open** list.

**All**, **Open** and **Answered** filter the list.

Writing, resolving and reopening comments needs a role that may edit the entry. Anyone who can read
the entry can read its comments, including after the entry is deleted.

## Tasks

A task is work that still has to be done before an entry is ready: *Add the oven temperature to the
method*. It is on one entry, may be waiting on one member of the project, and is **open**, **done** or
**cancelled**.

### Make a task

<Steps>

1. Open **Tasks** in the sidebar, under **Work**, and press **New task**.

2. Fill in **What has to be done** (up to 200 characters) and choose the entry it is **On**. *Only
   entries your role may change are listed.*

3. Optionally choose who it is **Waiting on** (*A member of this project, or nobody yet.*), and the
   exact **On version** or **On publish request** it is about.

4. Press **Create task**.

</Steps>

### The Tasks page

![The Tasks page: the All, Open, Done and Cancelled filters, Waiting on Anyone, and three tasks with the entry each is on, who it waits on, its status and when it was updated](/screens/studio/tasks.webp)

The table shows each task, the entry it is **On**, who it is **Waiting on** (**You** for yourself),
its **Status** and when it was **Updated**. Filter by status with **All**, **Open**, **Done** and
**Cancelled**, and by person with **Waiting on**: **Anyone**, **Me** or **Nobody**.

Press a task to open it. There you can:

- rename it with **Rename**;
- change who it is **Waiting on**, or set it to **Unassigned**;
- press **Mark done**, **Cancel task**, or **Reopen** a finished one;
- read and write the entry's **Comments** without leaving the page.

A task that is done says who finished it. If someone changed the task while you had it open, your
change is refused with *Someone changed this task since you loaded it. Look at it again, then try once
more.*, so nobody's update is lost.

Making and changing tasks needs a role that may edit the entry. A task can only wait on a member of the
project.

The project's **Overview** also gathers your own work: **Your drafts**, the publish requests
**Waiting for your review**, what is **Scheduled** and the **Recent releases**.
